HadyanCode periodically offers **free model promotions** that let you try select models at no cost, up to a limited usage quota. It's a simple way to explore HadyanCode and experiment with different models before choosing a paid option.

<Note>
Free model promotions are offered on a rotating, limited-time basis. The specific free models available may change over time.
</Note>

## How it works

Free models are available to any user with a HadyanCode account.

After you use your free model quota, you can continue coding with HadyanCode by switching to either:

- **[HadyanCodePass](/getting-started/hadyancodepass)** — a flat $9.99/month subscription with 2-5x API rate limits on popular open coding models
- **[HadyanCode (usage-billing)](/getting-started/hadyancode-provider)** — pay-as-you-go access with HadyanCode credits across 100+ models

Both options let you keep working after your free quota is exhausted.

## Where to find free models

You can find free models under **both** HadyanCode providers:

### HadyanCode (usage-billing)

In the HadyanCode (usage-billing) provider, look for models tagged **FREE** in the model selector.

- **IDE Extension**: Go to IDE extension settings, set **API Provider** to **HadyanCode**, and look for free-tagged models.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-vscode-hadyancode-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the HadyanCode provider in VS Code" />
</Frame>

- **CLI**: Go to `/settings`, select **HadyanCode** as your provider, and browse available free models.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-cli-hadyancode-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the HadyanCode provider in the HadyanCode CLI" />
</Frame>

### HadyanCodePass

During promotional periods, free models are also available through the HadyanCodePass provider.

- **IDE Extension**: Go to IDE extension settings, set **API Provider** to **HadyanCodePass**, and sign in.

  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-vscode-hadyancodepass-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the HadyanCodePass provider in VS Code" />

- **CLI**: Go to `/settings`, select **HadyanCodePass** as your provider.

## Important notes

### API access

Free model usage is **not supported through the HadyanCode API**. Free models are only available in the HadyanCode IDE Extension and CLI.

### Model improvements

Free model usage may be used to help improve model performance and quality.

## After your free quota

When your free quota runs out, you have several options:

| Option | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| [HadyanCodePass](/getting-started/hadyancodepass) ($9.99/mo) | Frequent users who want reliable access to curated open models with higher rate limits |
| [HadyanCode (usage-billing)](/getting-started/hadyancode-provider) | Flexible, pay-as-you-go access across 100+ models |
| [Bring your own API key](/provider-config/anthropic) | Users who already have API keys from other providers |
